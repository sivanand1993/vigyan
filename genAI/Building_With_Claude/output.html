# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Training Athlete

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete training for a marathon with carbohydrate-loading for race day:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 4500 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (135g), Fat (120g), Carbs (630g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Pre-Run Breakfast (6:00 AM):** Bagel (85g) with jam (30g) and banana (150g). Orange juice (250ml).
    * Protein: 10g, Fat: 2g, Carbs: 105g
    * *Timing: Light, easily digestible carbs for 2-hour morning run*

* **Post-Run Snack (8:30 AM):** Greek yogurt (200g, plain) with granola (50g) and berries (100g). Dates (60g).
    * Protein: 20g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 85g
    * *Timing: Immediate recovery post-run; protein + carbs for glycogen replenishment*

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:30 AM):** Whole wheat toast (2 slices, 60g) with chickpea spread (50g). Apple (180g).
    * Protein: 12g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 65g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Vegetarian pasta (150g cooked) with marinara sauce (200g), lentil bolognese (150g cooked), and side salad with olive oil dressing (1 tbsp/15g). Whole wheat bread (60g).
    * Protein: 28g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 110g

* **Pre-Strength Session Snack (3:30 PM):** Rice cakes (40g) with peanut butter alternative - sunflower seed butter (40g). Honey (20g). Coconut water (250ml).
    * Protein: 12g, Fat: 16g, Carbs: 85g
    * *Timing: 1.5 hours before afternoon strength session*

* **Dinner (6:30 PM):** Baked tofu (200g) with roasted sweet potato (250g), steamed broccoli (150g), and tahini drizzle (15g). Brown rice (120g cooked).
    * Protein: 30g, Fat: 38g, Carbs: 120g

* **Evening Snack (8:30 PM):** Oatmeal (50g dry) cooked with almond milk (200ml), topped with banana (120g) and cinnamon.
    * Protein: 8g, Fat: 3g, Carbs: 75g

* **Late Evening Snack (10:00 PM):** Whole grain crackers (40g) with hummus (60g).
    * Protein: 7g, Fat: 6g, Carbs: 20g

---

**Key Features of This Plan:**
- **Carbohydrate-focused** (630g) to maximize glycogen stores for marathon training
- **Strategically timed meals** around your 2-hour morning run (light pre-run, substantial post-run) and afternoon strength session
- **Vegetarian protein sources** include Greek yogurt, tofu, lentils, legumes, and whole grains
- **No nuts:** Uses seeds (sunflower butter, tahini) as alternatives
- **Budget-friendly:** Relies on affordable staples like oats, rice, pasta, lentils, and seasonal produce
